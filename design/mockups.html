The body map, rebuilt from your reference — and a header bug fixed

Seventeen screens. The body map is redrawn end to end around your new reference image, with the coiled intestine as the clear centerpiece. Daily Check-in's step titles no longer risk wrapping to two lines on a real device, and the Pain screen's two chip rows are now one.

What's new in this pass:
  • Breathing exercises footer now reads "A moment to pause, reset, and breathe" instead of the more clinical disclaimer phrasing
  • Fixed title wrapping on every Daily Check-in step (1 of 5 through 5 of 5) — the screen title now sits on its own full-width line below the back/step-counter row, instead of being squeezed between them where it could wrap to two lines, as you saw happen with "School & home life"
  • Body map rebuilt around your reference: arms now hang close to the body instead of angled out, small knee accents on the legs, dashed callout lines, and — the main change — a large, densely coiled rust-orange intestine mass as the dominant feature, with the smaller organs (stomach, liver, gallbladder, spleen) sitting above it rather than mixed in
  • Pain screen's chips combined — "What kind of pain?" and "Other symptoms with this pain?" are now one "What's it like?" row
